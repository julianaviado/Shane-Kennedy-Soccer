Shane Kennedy

CIF Coach of the Year CIF Model Coach Prep2Prep NCS Coach of the Year

Coach Shane has trained over 500 athletes across 10+ club and high school teams in Marin County since 2008. As Head Coach at Tam High, Assistant Coach at Dominican University, record-setting NCAA goalkeeper, and NY Cosmos draft pick, he provides the 1-on-1 focus team settings miss.

Whether coaching field players or specialized keepers, his focus never changes: building complete players through real skill development, tactical intelligence, and mental toughness.
