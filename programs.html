How every session is built

Four pillars, every position.

Every player Coach Shane trains develops across the same four areas, with a deep specialty in goalkeeping.

Position-specific training

Technical

Command of the Box

Master footwork to attack high balls at their highest point, execute clean catch-or-punch decisions, and dominate the six-yard box with total air authority.

Technical

Footwork

Build quick, balanced steps to set behind the ball in time, sharpening the footwork foundation that makes elite saves possible.

Technical

Distribution

Beat the press with composed short passing and break lines with precision long distribution, the core skill college coaches evaluate first.

Tactical

Angles & the Set

Cut down shooter angles, master a balanced set position before every strike, and read the play early to be set before the ball is struck.

Tactical

1-v-1 & Breakaways

Close space fast, stay big through the strike, and time the spread to dominate high-stakes breakaway moments with total intention.

Game Intelligence

Reading the Play

Anticipate the danger: starting position and depth behind a high line, sweeping in behind, and commanding the box before the ball arrives.

Mentality

The Reset & Mental Game

Develop a rock-solid pre-shot routine, master a fast reset ritual after goals, and build the vocal command needed to direct your back line under pressure.

Defenders

Back line

Positioning, 1-v-1 defending, clean first touch under pressure, and playing out of the back with composure.

Midfield

The engine

Receiving on the half-turn, scanning before the ball arrives, passing range, and controlling tempo in both directions.

Attackers

Final third

First touch into space, finishing with both feet, movement off the ball, and the decision-making that creates chances.
